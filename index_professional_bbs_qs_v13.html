Code basis used by this BBS workflow
IS 2502 — bending and fixing of reinforcement bars. BIS reference
SP 34 — concrete reinforcement detailing and preparation of bar bending schedules. BIS reference
IS 456 — plain and reinforced concrete; detailing inputs such as cover/development/lap are project/design dependent.
IS 1786 — high-strength deformed steel bars and wires for concrete reinforcement.
The app does not invent structural design values. Development length, lap length, cover and anchorage must follow the approved structural design/drawings and applicable standard provisions.
BBS reference conventions
45° bend: 1D · 90° bend: 2D · 135° bend: 3D · 180° bend: 4D · Hook: 10D
These labels follow the supplied workbook's BBS reference section.
